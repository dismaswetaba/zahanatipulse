# ZahanatiPulse

### Smart Healthcare Management and Patient Flow System

ZahanatiPulse is a web-based healthcare management platform designed to streamline healthcare facility operations through digital patient management, efficient service workflows, and AI-powered operational insights.

## Overview

Healthcare facilities often face challenges such as inefficient patient registration, long waiting times, poor appointment coordination, difficulties monitoring medicine inventory, and limited access to operational data.

ZahanatiPulse aims to address these challenges by providing a centralized platform for managing essential healthcare operations, improving patient flow, and supporting data-driven decision-making.

## Key Features

- **Patient Management** – Digital patient registration, record management, and patient search.
- **Appointment Management** – Scheduling, tracking, and managing appointments.
- **Queue Management** – Digital queues, patient calling, and service status tracking.
- **Medicine Inventory** – Stock monitoring, stock movement tracking, and low-stock alerts.
- **Operational Dashboard** – Real-time overview of facility activities.
- **Reports and Analytics** – Operational statistics and performance summaries.
- **AI Assistant** – Natural-language access to facility operational information.
- **AI Analytics** – AI-powered analysis of operational trends, patient flow, and inventory data.

## User Roles

- **Administrator** – Manages facility users, system settings, and reports.
- **Receptionist** – Handles patient registration, appointments, and queues.
- **Clinician** – Accesses authorized patient information and service workflows.
- **Pharmacist** – Manages medicine inventory and stock movements.

## Multi-Facility Support

ZahanatiPulse is designed to support multiple healthcare facilities within a centralized system while maintaining facility-specific users, patient records, appointments, inventory, and operational data.

## Security & Privacy

The system is designed with role-based access control, secure authentication, facility-level data isolation, and protected database access in mind. AI services will be restricted to authorized operational information and will not be used for medical diagnosis or treatment decisions.

**The development and hackathon prototype will use fictional patient data only.**
